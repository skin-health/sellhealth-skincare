# Derm Audit Journal | Clinical Skincare & Beauty Affiliate Portal

This website is a dermatological product review and clinical comparison portal. It analyzes evidence-based skincare, anti-aging, nutricosmetic, and targeted recovery products from SellHealth and ClickBank.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **5 In-Depth Clinical Reviews**: Unbiased biochemical breakdowns, clinical trial highlights, and relatable real-world analogies for:
  1. **Kollagen Intensiv™** (Top Daily Anti-Aging Cream - Syn-Coll® +354% Collagen)
  2. **Synevra UltraLift™** (Top Expression Line Serum - SYN-AKE® 82% Muscle Relaxation)
  3. **Illuminatural 6i™** (Top Skin Lightener - 6 Non-Bleach Botanical Brighteners)
  4. **Eyelasticity™** (Top Eye Bag & Dark Circle Therapy - Eyeseryl® & Regu®-Age)
  5. **Dermefface FX7®** (Top Scar Remodeling Therapy - Type I Collagen +1,190%)
* **Interactive Clinical Comparison Matrix**: Multi-category matchup tables evaluating key actives, guarantees, delivery models, and clinical pricing against luxury drugstore alternatives.
* **Smart 3-Step Skin Assessment Quiz**: Interactive JavaScript matching engine that analyzes concern, daily routine preference, and age to recommend the exact product with direct discount links and review access.
* **Social Bio-Link Portal (`links.html`)**: Mobile-optimized link-in-bio hub with quick access to all clinical reviews and discounts.
* **SEO & LLM Optimized**: Valid JSON-LD Schema (MedicalWebPage, Product, Review), OpenGraph, Twitter Cards, `sitemap.xml` (49 indexable URLs), and `llms.txt`.
* **Affiliate Security & Hardening**: Centralized tracking in `app.js` with HTTPS enforcement and `noopener nofollow sponsored` link hardening.
