What on Earth Is a Skin Tag?

Let's be totally honest: waking up and finding a weird little flap of extra skin hanging out on your neck, armpit, or chest is not the vibe.

Here is the simple truth: a skin tag is basically like wired earbuds getting tangled inside your backpack pocket.

When two skin surfaces constantly rub against each other (like your shirt collar rubbing your neck, or friction in your armpits), your body tries to protect itself. But instead of laying down smooth, flat skin, tiny strands of collagen and micro-blood vessels get twisted into a tight knot. Over time, that knot pushes upward into a harmless, but super annoying, little flap.

⚠️ Why You Should NEVER Cut Them Yourself:
Searching YouTube for "how to cut off skin tags at home" will show you terrifying DIY hacks with nail clippers, scissors, dental floss, or burning acids. Please don't do that! That causes severe bleeding, intense stinging, bacterial infections, and permanent dark scars that look 10x worse than the tag itself.

How ReviTag™ Untangles the Knot

Instead of burning the skin off with aggressive chemicals or freezing nitrogen, ReviTag™ takes the gentle, high-IQ route.

When you apply a few drops directly to the skin tag with the precision dropper, its concentrated botanical oils seep right down to the root of the twisted collagen bundle. It calms the friction signals, soothes the underlying tissue, and gradually deprives the tag of excess moisture. Within a few weeks, the tag naturally shrivels, turns into a tiny dry flake, and slips away unnoticed in the shower.
