Dermefface FX7 Review 2026: Does Scar Remodeling Therapy Actually Work?

By Derm Glow Editorial Team • Updated August 2026 • 6 Min Read

Whether from surgery, injury, burns, or severe acne, scars represent a complex biological response to skin trauma. When the dermis is damaged, the body rushes to close the wound, often producing chaotic, disorganized collagen structures that result in raised, hard, or discolored scar tissue.

Conventional scar gels rely purely on silicone sheets to seal moisture. While hydration helps, they fail to actively accelerate cellular remodeling. Skinception Dermefface FX7™ Scar Reduction Therapy takes a biological approach, using active signaling peptides and moisture complexes to speed skin healing and normalize collagen production.

The Science of Scar Remodeling

Dermefface FX7 operates in three phases to remodel damaged skin tissue:

  1. Inflammatory Phase Control: Regulates cell signaling to reduce redness, swelling, and itching.
  2. Remodeling Phase Acceleration: Stimulates healthy cell turnover to push damaged cells to the surface.
  3. Collagen Normalization: Shifts the ratio of collagen production to ensure smooth, flat, elastic skin instead of hard scar fibers.

The Key Active Ingredients

1. Symglucan (10.0% Concentration): The Healing Accelerator

Symglucan is a oat-derived beta-glucan fluid. It penetrates the skin barrier to stimulate fibroblasts, the cells responsible for collagen and tissue synthesis. Symglucan speeds skin healing, reduces the visible height of hypertrophic scars, and decreases skin redness.

2. Pro-Coll-One+ (2.0% Concentration): The Type I Collagen Stimulator

Pro-Coll-One+ is a soy glycopeptide complex. It targets fibroblasts to increase synthesis of Type I collagen—the primary structural protein in healthy skin. In clinical testing, Pro-Coll-One+ increased Type I collagen synthesis by up to 1190%, allowing the skin to replace disorganized scar fibers with a smooth matrix.

3. Pentavitin (5.0% Concentration): The Moisture Lock

Pentavitin (Saccharide Isomerate) is a plant-derived carbohydrate complex that binds to keratin in the stratum corneum. It forms a natural moisture reservoir that cannot be washed off easily. This constant hydration softens the fibrous tissue of the scar and relieves the associated itching.

4. Vitalayer (3.0% Concentration): The Skin Renewer

Vitalayer is extracted from Verbena officinalis and is rich in active peptides. It promotes healthy epidermal differentiation and accelerates skin barrier healing. It also helps to normalize pigment levels, reducing dark scar discoloration.

Complete Active Ingredient List

  • Symglucan (10.0%): Accelerates cellular healing and reduces scar thickness.
  • DL-Panthenol (8.0%): Prevents hypertrophic/keloid scar formation and calms inflammation.
  • Pentavitin (5.0%): Binds moisture to dry, hard scar tissue to soften the texture.
  • Vitalayer (3.0%): Regulates cell turnover and reduces dark spot/red spot discoloration.
  • Pro-Coll-One+ (2.0%): Stimulates healthy Type I collagen synthesis in dermal fibroblasts.
  • Allantoin & Niacinamide: Calms irritation, prevents skin redness, and fades post-acne scarring.
