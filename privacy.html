Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Derm Glow Journal, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Derm Glow Journal and how we use it.

1. General Information Collection

We do not collect any personal identifying information (PII) from you when you browse our site, unless you explicitly contact us or sign up for a newsletter. We use standard browser local storage and cookies to save preferences, such as your interactive skin assessment quiz results.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Derm Glow Journal uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Derm Glow Journal is an affiliate marketing website. We feature products from third-party networks, specifically SellHealth. These cookies are subject to the respective privacy policies of the merchants:

  • SellHealth: Privacy policy and tracking details are available on their official merchant portal.

4. Third Party Privacy Policies

Derm Glow Journal's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. GDPR and CCPA Rights

We respect your privacy rights. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data cached in your browser. You can clear your browser's cookies and cache at any time to delete all stored site data.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@dermglowjournal.com.
