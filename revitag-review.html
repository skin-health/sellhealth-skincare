What on Earth Is a Skin Tag? (Explain Like I'm 15)

Let's be totally honest: waking up and finding a weird little flap of extra skin hanging out on your neck, armpit, or chest is not the vibe.

Here is the simple truth: a skin tag is basically like wired earbuds getting tangled inside your backpack pocket.

When two skin surfaces constantly rub against each other (like your collar rubbing your neck, or friction in your armpit), your body tries to shield itself. But instead of laying down flat, smooth skin, tiny fibers of collagen and blood vessels get twisted into a microscopic knot. Over time, that knot pushes upward and forms a harmless, but super annoying, little flap.

⚠️ Why You Should NEVER Cut Them Yourself:
Searching YouTube for *"how to cut off skin tags at home"* will show you scary hacks with nail clippers, dental floss, or burning acids. Please don't do that! That causes bleeding, intense pain, nasty infections, and permanent scarring that looks 10x worse than the tag itself.
