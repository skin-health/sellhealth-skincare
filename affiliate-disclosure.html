3. How Commercial Partnerships Affect Editorial Audits

We disclose our commercial relationships transparently. Our formula evaluations and evidence scores are determined according to the criteria set forth in our published Audit Methodology, which examines INCI ingredient lists, published biochemical literature, concentration disclosures, and excipient suitability.

A product reviewed on this site can receive a low score, receive critical commentary, or be highlighted for formula limitations regardless of whether an affiliate partnership exists. We do not claim that this publication is completely unbiased, ad-free, or detached from commercial interests; rather, we provide structured, cited ingredient analysis so readers can make informed choices based on documented data.

4. Manufacturer Guarantees and Refund Handling

Every product reviewed on this site is backed by a manufacturer money-back guarantee, typically ranging from 60 to 90 days (for example, 90 days for Skinception products, 60 days for Synevra UltraLift, and 67 days for Eyelasticity). These guarantee windows and return conditions are determined and administered exclusively by the respective manufacturers.

Derm Audit Journal is an editorial publication and does not sell products, collect billing information, or process returns. The site does not issue refunds. To initiate a refund under a manufacturer's guarantee, please contact the customer support team of the merchant through whom your order was placed, as indicated on your order receipt.
