The Dermal Remodeling Cycle: How Symglucan & Peptides Flatten Scar Matrix

August 4, 2026 7 Min Read Clinical Review

Scar formation is a natural biological response to deep dermal trauma (surgery, acne lesions, burns, or lacerations). However, when damaged skin heals, fibroblasts often deposit collagen in a disorganized, rigid lattice. This results in raised keloids, pitted ice-pick scars, or persistent hyperpigmentation.

1. The 3 Phases of Scar Remodeling

Understanding how topical scar therapies function requires breaking down the wound healing cycle:

  • Inflammatory Phase (Days 1–6): Histamines increase vascular permeability. Excess inflammatory signaling can trigger hypertrophic overgrowths.
  • Proliferative Phase (Days 7–21): Fibroblasts rapidly produce Type III collagen to close the wound.
  • Remodeling Phase (Day 28 to 2 Years): Disorganized collagen is gradually broken down and replaced with structured, elastic Type I collagen.

2. Key Remodeling Actives: Symglucan & Pro-Coll-One+

Standard silicone gels only provide passive moisture locking. Advanced therapeutic scar formulas combine passive occlusion with active biological signaling agents:

  • Symglucan (10.0% Concentration): Derived from oat beta-glucan, Symglucan penetrates deep into the dermis to stimulate macrophage activity and accelerate cellular turnover, pushing scarred cells to the surface.
  • Pro-Coll-One+ (2.0% Concentration): Highly purified soy glycopeptides that increase healthy Type I collagen synthesis in dermal fibroblasts by up to 1190%.
  • DL-Panthenol (8.0%) & Pentavitin (5.0%): Binds deep hydration to hard scar tissue, relieving tightness and itching while calming erythema.

3. Clinical Recommendation

For maximum scar flattening and discoloration fading, therapy should begin as soon as the wound is fully closed. Clinical testing shows that 7-active formulas like Dermefface FX7™ produce visible flattening and texture smoothing within 28 to 80 days.
