Why Is Nail Fungus So Annoyingly Hard to Kill?

Have you ever had an uninvited party crasher who eats all your chips, ruins the music, and simply refuses to leave your house no matter what you say?

That is nail fungus in a nutshell.

Fungal spores love dark, warm, sweaty environments (like inside your sneakers after gym class). Once they find a microscopic crack in your nail, they burrow underneath your hard keratin shield.

Here is where most people get tricked: they buy a cheap drugstore cream, use it for 4 days until the itching stops, and then celebrate and quit. Big mistake! The fungus wasn't actually dead—it was just stunned. When you stop early, the surviving fungus mutates and levels up like a video game boss, becoming completely immune to that cream.

Scientists from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control (CDC) proved that fungal resistance is why 80%+ of people struggle with yellow, crumbly nails for years. To win, you need a formula that attacks the fungus from 4 different biological angles at once, so it can never evolve immunity.

How Kerassentials Penetrates the Keratin Armor

Nails are made of dense keratin proteins. Standard creams are water-based, which means they literally slide right off the surface like water off a duck's back.

Kerassentials™ is formulated as an ultra-light botanical oil blend. Because oil easily dissolves through the lipid layers of nail keratin, it acts like a high-tech delivery vehicle—driving the active ingredients all the way down into the bed where the fungus is hiding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply Kerassentials?

Use the enclosed brush applicator to paint the oil across the entire nail plate, and use a cotton swab to gently press the liquid under the nail tip and surrounding cuticle. Apply 4 times daily for optimal results.

Can I wear colored nail polish while using Kerassentials?

It is best to leave your nails bare during treatment. Heavy nail polishes trap moisture beneath the lacquer, creating a sauna-like breeding ground for fungus. Kerassentials gives nails a clean, natural sheen on its own!
