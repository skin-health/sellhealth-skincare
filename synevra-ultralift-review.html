Synevra UltraLift Review 2026: Does SYN-AKE® Dipeptide Smooth Expression Lines?

By Derm Audit Editorial Team • Updated August 2026 • 7 Min Read

Dynamic wrinkles—such as crow's feet around the lateral canthus, glabellar frown lines between the brows, and horizontal forehead furrows—are triggered by repetitive facial muscle contractions. Over decades, these micro-tensions crease the dermal matrix, creating permanent folds as elastin and collagen reserves deplete.

While neurotoxin injections temporarily freeze facial muscles, many seeking non-invasive options turn to Synevra UltraLift™. Marketed as a Swiss-inspired "Lift & Lock" protocol, this system combines a high-potency topical serum with oral beauty nutrients. In this clinical evaluation, we analyze its molecular mechanism, safety profile, and real-world efficacy.

What Is the Synevra UltraLift™ System?

Synevra UltraLift is a dual-action anti-aging system engineered to target wrinkle formation from both external and internal pathways:

  • Topical UltraLift Serum: Formulated with patented SYN-AKE® dipeptide, Sodium Hyaluronate, and antioxidant vitamins to relax surface micro-contractions and plump the epidermis.
  • VitaLock™ Oral Beauty Nutrients: A specialized dietary supplement containing Biotin, Niacinamide, Zinc, Vitamin B6, and Horsetail Extract to provide essential building blocks for structural skin repair.

The Science of Neuromuscular Smoothing: SYN-AKE® Peptide

The primary active compound in the UltraLift Serum is SYN-AKE® (Dipeptide Diaminobutyroyl Benzylamide Diacetate), a synthetic tripeptide derivative inspired by the neuromuscular blocking mechanism of Temple Viper venom (*Tropidolaemus wagleri*).

1. Mechanism of Action at Acetylcholine Receptors

In normal muscle contractions, nerve terminals release the neurotransmitter acetylcholine (ACh) into the synaptic cleft. ACh binds to post-synaptic nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChR) on muscle cells, causing sodium ion (Na+) influx and muscle cell contraction.

SYN-AKE® acts as a competitive antagonist at nAChR. By occupying these receptor sites, it impedes sodium channel opening and suppresses micro-contractions in facial expression muscles. Unlike surgical neurotoxin injections, SYN-AKE® produces a gentle, localized smoothing effect without freezing facial expressions or compromising natural movement.

Clinical Trial Highlight:

In in-vitro and controlled clinical trials, formulations containing 4% SYN-AKE® demonstrated a 52% reduction in visible forehead wrinkle depth and up to an 82% reduction in facial muscle micro-contractions after 28 days of continuous application.

2. Multi-Depth Epidermal Plumping & Antioxidants

In addition to SYN-AKE®, the topical serum delivers three targeted active agents:

  • Sodium Hyaluronate: Low-molecular humectant that penetrates the upper stratum corneum to bind up to 1,000 times its molecular weight in moisture, restoring immediate turgor and bounce.
  • Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C): Neutralizes reactive oxygen species (ROS), protects existing collagen fibers from photo-degradation, and brightens dull areas.
  • Tocopheryl Acetate (Vitamin E): Lipophilic antioxidant that reinforces lipid bilayer membranes and stabilizes Vitamin C activity.

The VitaLock™ Internal Complex: Systemic Support

Topical treatments act on the upper 100–200 microns of skin. Synevra addresses systemic skin nutrition through its oral VitaLock™ formulation:

  • Biotin & Niacinamide (Vitamin B3): Accelerates keratin synthesis and supports cellular ceramide production to prevent transepidermal water loss (TEWL).
  • Horsetail Extract (*Equisetum arvense*): Rich in bioavailable organic silica (orthosilicic acid), an essential cofactor for prolyl hydroxylase enzymes involved in collagen cross-linking.
  • Zinc Oxide: Supports cellular division, tissue repair, and regulates sebum balance.
