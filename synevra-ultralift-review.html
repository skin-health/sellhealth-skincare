Why Do Expression Lines Turn Into Permanent Creases?

Grab a clean sheet of printer paper and fold it in half once. If you unfold it right away, the paper stays pretty smooth.

Now imagine folding and creasing that exact same spot 50,000 times. Eventually, the paper fibers snap, and you get a deep permanent groove that never goes away.

That is exactly what happens to your face. Every time you squint at your phone screen in bright sunlight, laugh at a meme, or furrow your eyebrows during a hard test, tiny facial muscles pull your skin into the exact same fold. As you get older, your skin loses the elastic bounce to snap back, turning "laugh lines" into permanent forehead trenches.
